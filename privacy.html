Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Global Crimson Education Hub ("we", "us") provides study-abroad counselling services from Biratchowk, Morang, Nepal. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our enquiry form or WhatsApp (name, address, phone number, course interest, joining date).
  • Basic analytics about how visitors use this site (pages viewed, approximate location, device type).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a free consultation.
  • To advise you on university, scholarship and visa options.
  • To improve this website and our services.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We may share application-related documents with universities, scholarship bodies or visa authorities strictly as part of processing your own application, and only with your knowledge.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing crimsonbiratchowk@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to crimsonbiratchowk@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.
